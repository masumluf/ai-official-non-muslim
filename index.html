About

What the product is built to solve

The platform is designed to reduce confusion, paperwork delays, and fragmented records during estate planning and estate administration.

What it does

Turns a complex planning process into a guided digital workflow with clear prompts, structured records, and trackable document status.

Why it matters

Families and executors often deal with scattered information, unclear instructions, and repeated manual follow-up. The platform centralizes the work.

Who it serves

Households preparing a will, professionals reviewing estate documents, and teams managing secure record retention over time.

Platform

How the workflow is structured

The current direction centers on intake, review, storage, and controlled access instead of static documents passed around by email.

Core capabilities

  • Questionnaire-driven data collection for estate planning inputs
  • Document generation workflows with status visibility
  • Secure record storage with audit-friendly history
  • Reviewer collaboration for lawyers, accountants, or advisors
  • Operational controls for staged rollout and managed access

Typical journey

  1. Create an account and enter household details.
  2. Complete guided prompts for beneficiaries, assets, and preferences.
  3. Generate draft documents and supporting records.
  4. Share with a reviewer or advisor for final checks.
  5. Maintain a durable digital record for future updates.
