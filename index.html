Practice Solutions

Precision-led solutions for complex estate practice delivery.

Built for succession law matters, technical frameworks, custom testamentary structures, and regulatory precision across modern estate departments.

Core feature set

  • Specialized Multi-Framework Succession Planning for complex succession law mandates
  • Custom Testamentary Trust Architectures for tailored asset protection objectives
  • Regulatory & Statutory Alignment across technical frameworks and distribution mandates
  • Scale: support for high-volume estate departments and expanding member firms
  • Precision: automated workflows to eliminate clerical errors in complex wills
  • Security: professional-grade data handling for sensitive client files

Delivery workflow

  1. Configure branded intake, reviewer roles, and seamless workflow integration for practice-specific matter controls.
  2. Route instructions through specialized succession planning or testamentary trust workflows.
  3. Generate draft documents through automated workflows designed to reduce clerical error.
  4. Review for regulatory and statutory alignment before final client delivery.
  5. Trigger revenue-share billing only when the client invoice event occurs.
